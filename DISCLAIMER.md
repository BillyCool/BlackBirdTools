# Disclaimer

## Purpose

These tools exist for **interoperability and preservation purposes** — specifically, to allow the NieR Re[in]carnation client to connect to an independently developed private server. The official service shut down in 2024, and the game cannot be played officially in any form.

## In-App Purchases

Some patches change the game's in-app purchase code. They are **not** intended for piracy and do not unlock or bypass any paid content from the official service. The official store and its Google Play billing backend no longer exist, so the game cannot complete or even start a purchase on its own. The patches only let purchase flows reach the private server. No real-money transactions are possible or circumvented.
